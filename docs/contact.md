## 🧾 Requesting a Report or Data Extract

1. Check the [Master Report Catalog](projects/daily-routine.md) first — it may already exist.
2. Submit via the internal ticket channel with: **question**, **BU**, **date range**, **deadline**.
3. Standard requests: **2 business days** · Executive briefings: **same day**.

## 🚨 Escalation (ETL / Data Issues)

| Severity | Example | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Low | One product unmapped | Log for Monday data-quality meeting |
| Medium | Match rate < 9% | Rebuild bridge — [Runbook ](policies/etl-runbook.md) |
| High | ETL failed 3× / revenue wrong | Page BI Lead + open incident |
