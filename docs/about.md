# 👥 Who We Are

## About Marathon Myanmar

**Marathon Myanmar Co., Ltd** is a tech-enabled logistics company providing **last-mile
delivery, warehousing and payment-collection services** to SMEs nationwide. What began
with two founders and three employees has grown into a **100+ person team**, owned by
Myanmar shareholders and Singapore-based **Seed Myanmar Venture Limited** (a Vulpes fund),
and advised by board members with international experience in investment banking,
logistics and business management.

!!! quote "Our Vision"
    To build a last mile and cash-on-delivery platform which connects delivery
    networks across Myanmar.
